# 林小羊角色母版

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one original Chinese internet chat-sticker character design master, a little sheep called Lin Xiaoyang. SINGLE full body sheep, no text. Personality: slightly overwhelmed and physically floppy, yet present and friendly; quietly funny, approachable without demanding a response. Simple 2D hand-drawn ink doodle with confident slightly uneven thick dark charcoal strokes, flat warm ivory wool, oatmeal bare face, tiny charcoal hooves and a pair of small beige curled horns, long relaxed sideways ears. Entire squat woolly body is one soft irregular cloud mass with only 5-7 big lobes, not detailed curls. Tiny wide-set dot eyes with a slight tired upper lid, tiny sideways mouth: neutral-friendly deadpan, not depressed, angry, smug or saccharine. No big shiny eyes, no blush, no 3D, no clay rendering, no gradients, no texture noise or deliberate pixelation. Slouching low seated posture with short legs splayed and one small hoof weakly raised in acknowledgement; oversized wool body with small face inset in upper third, simple silhouette recognizable at 50px. The humorous defining behavior is that this sheep compresses like a soft cushion when tired but still raises one hoof to respond. No costume, no bow, no slogans, no props, no decorative emblem. Square canvas true transparent background, generous 8 percent safe margin. Clean legible organic drawing, minimal two-tone palette, an original useful reaction character rather than a decorative holiday mascot.
