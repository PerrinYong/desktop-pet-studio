# 好耶 循环关键帧

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE 2x2 animation sprite sheet for the SAME Lin Xiaoyang sheep from reference, for a looping Chinese chat GIF. This is animation artwork, not a poster. Square canvas divided into FOUR EXACTLY EQUAL square cells, row-major order. NO visible grid, dividers, frames, labels or text. TRUE TRANSPARENT background across whole sheet. Each cell contains exactly ONE complete sheep, comfortably inset by at least 12 percent from its cell boundary; absolutely no crossing cell boundaries. Keep character identity identical across cells: warm ivory cloud wool, oatmeal face, two beige curl horns, sideways ears, tiny dot eyes, short dark hooves, thick charcoal loose 2D doodle outlines and a thin ivory sticker cutline. No 3D, detailed texture, extra characters or props. Same drawing scale and camera across cells; change actual body deformation and limb pose, not simply translate a rigid drawing. Baseline y=85 percent in each cell for grounded poses. White cutline and dark stroke must remain readable. Four animation key poses in sequence: TOP LEFT anticipation: sheep crouches into a very low broad wool cushion, arms bent near body, pleased eyes. TOP RIGHT takeoff: sheep stretches tall springing off ground, both hooves raise, legs extend downward, simple open joyful mouth. BOTTOM LEFT peak: sheep fully airborne higher in cell, both arms high in uneven V and legs tucked, fluffy body tall, ears high, same joyful mouth. BOTTOM RIGHT landing: sheep drops into a rounded low body with knees bent and arms halfway down, happy smile. Avoid all letters/symbols. Each pose must be distinct but recognizable as the same elastic sheep.
