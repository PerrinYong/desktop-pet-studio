# 守岁中

Use case: stylized-concept. Character identity reference ONLY. Draw a FOUR-POSE animated chat sticker sheet of the SAME Lin Xiaoyang sheep. Strict 2x2 grid of four equally-sized square cells on square canvas, in row-major order. Invisible cell divisions. TRUE TRANSPARENT ALPHA background; absolutely NO white/gray rectangle, checkerboard, cast shadows, scenery, labels, letters, Chinese writing or watermark. Each cell ONE complete sheep with its described props; all content inset 12% from cell edges, nothing crosses seams. Same head size, drawing scale, camera and grounded baseline at 82% cell height. Warm ivory cloud wool, oatmeal face, two beige curled horns, sideways floppy ears, tiny dot eyes, small dark hooves, thick charcoal slightly uneven outlines with THIN ivory cutline, simple warm 2D doodle with minimal shading. Preserve horn/ear identity and short expressive limbs. Actual changes in pose and expression, NOT rigid translation. Lunar New Year warmth and low-pressure humor. Four poses TOP LEFT, TOP RIGHT, BOTTOM LEFT, BOTTOM RIGHT: Sheep wearing festive red scarf, sitting with ONE tiny red lantern on floor beside it, lantern fixed in position every cell, no symbols. 1 sitting upright with tired half-open eyes, front hooves on knees. 2 eyelids close and head nods forward sleepily, wool body compresses. 3 suddenly catches itself and sits taller, startled open dot eyes, ears pop sideways, one tiny motion dash. 4 relaxes back to half-open eyes and low shoulders. No clocks, numerals, Z letters or fireworks.
