# 先吃饭哈

Use case: stylized-concept. Character identity reference ONLY. Draw a FOUR-POSE animated chat sticker sheet of the SAME Lin Xiaoyang sheep. Strict 2x2 grid of four equally-sized square cells on square canvas, in row-major order. Invisible cell divisions. TRUE TRANSPARENT ALPHA background; absolutely NO white/gray rectangle, checkerboard, cast shadows, scenery, labels, letters, Chinese writing or watermark. Each cell ONE complete sheep with its described props; all content inset 12% from cell edges, nothing crosses seams. Same head size, drawing scale, camera and grounded baseline at 82% cell height. Warm ivory cloud wool, oatmeal face, two beige curled horns, sideways floppy ears, tiny dot eyes, small dark hooves, thick charcoal slightly uneven outlines with THIN ivory cutline, simple warm 2D doodle with minimal shading. Preserve horn/ear identity and short expressive limbs. Actual changes in pose and expression, NOT rigid translation. Lunar New Year warmth and low-pressure humor. Four poses TOP LEFT, TOP RIGHT, BOTTOM LEFT, BOTTOM RIGHT: Sheep wearing tiny red festive scarf holding ONE plain rice bowl in LEFT front hoof AT THE SAME left chest position in all four cells. RIGHT hoof free, never switch hands, no utensils. 1 polite slightly sheepish smile, right hoof low. 2 raises right hoof open-palmed as gentle pause gesture, head tilts, no anger. 3 turns right hoof open toward the bowl inviting everyone to eat first, eyebrows gently hopeful and smile warm. 4 right hoof lowers beside bowl, soft relieved smile. No finger wagging, no other character, no extra hands.
