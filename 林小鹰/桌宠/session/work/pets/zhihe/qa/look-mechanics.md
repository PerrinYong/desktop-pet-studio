# Zhihe gaze construction

The two feet and pear-shaped torso stay planted and front-facing. The distinct clay head turns at the neck, with small ear-tip follow-through. Its ivory facial disks, amber iris surfaces, black pupils and amber beak turn together as physical features; eyes are never replaced or detached.

Up: head pitches back, beak moves above its neutral head-center-relative position, amber irises and pupils visibly aim upward, while the face remains readable.
Right: head yaws toward screen-right and the beak projects to viewer-right of the head center; pupils also aim right. Torso stays forward.
Down: head pitches toward its chest, beak aims down below its neutral position, upper gray head becomes more visible, eyelids follow downward gaze. Keep both eyes identifiable.
Left: mirror the right head mechanics toward screen-left without turning the whole body.

Intermediates smoothly combine both axes. Keep head size, body scale, wing attachments, feet baseline, material and light stable across the sixteen-pose clockwise loop. No whole-character turntable. No spinning, rolling, replacement pupils, props, symbols or detached effects.
